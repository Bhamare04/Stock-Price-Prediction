# Limitations

- The model is based on historical market behavior and is primarily trained on AAPL.
- A ticker must have its own exported model and scaler; the system never reuses another ticker's artifacts.
- Markets are uncertain and data may be delayed, unavailable, or changed by Yahoo Finance.
- Technical indicators are descriptive, not guarantees of future movement.
- This academic system does not provide financial advice or execute trades.
- Model artifacts and metrics are unavailable until `python ml/train_model.py` completes.
