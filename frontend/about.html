ABOUT THE SYSTEM

An explainable
academic project.

OBJECTIVE

Market context, not certainty.

Market Lens combines real Yahoo Finance data, classical technical indicators, and a TensorFlow LSTM to study next-day closing-price prediction.

METHOD

AAPL / 60 days / LSTM

The model uses scaled Close prices, two 50-unit LSTM layers, dropout, and a chronological evaluation split. It is trained and evaluated primarily on AAPL history.

BOUNDARIES

No trading layer.

This system does not connect to brokerages, execute trades, or provide guaranteed investment advice. Predictions are estimates based on historical data.

Past performance does not guarantee future results.
